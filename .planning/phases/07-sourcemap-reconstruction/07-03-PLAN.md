<objective>
Two capability bans and one dependency relocation — the mechanical half of MAP-03 and MAP-04.

D-16 moves the VLQ decode to the browser, which removes the stall problem rather than gating around
it: SPIKE-06 measured `vlq_decode` at 167 ms on 8.3 MB against a 25 ms slice, `decode()` consumes the
whole `mappings` string so it cannot be chunked, and 167 ms of blocked QuickJS is 167 ms in which live
browsing is not being proxied. D-17 turns that into a package-level capability ban, because a
boundary a refactor can quietly move is the weaker kind of gate.

D-12 keeps the traversal fixtures — not to test a defence, but to prove that a `sources` entry never
reaches a path-like sink. MAP-04's first clause has no subject under D-07 (nothing is written); its
second clause is met by a gate that goes red the day a filesystem returns.

Purpose: MAP-03 and MAP-04 become mechanical properties rather than review findings.
Output: one manifest line, and two new members of the static-gate family.
</objective>
