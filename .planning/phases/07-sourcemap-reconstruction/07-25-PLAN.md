<objective>
Turn the two superseded figures in the documented-derivation block from expressions recomputed off
today's constants into named literals with stated provenance, and add the one-line assertion that
surfaces the day the coincidence between them ends.

This closes **G-07-8 / WR-04**, the one major and the one structural gap of round 3. Plan 07-18's
non-vacuity companion computes `128 + 3 + 2 * 2048 = 4,227` from TODAY's constants, but 4,227 is not
a property of today's constants — it is what the insert side READ before commit `59347c3`. Re-measure
`SOURCE_ROWS_PER_MAP_MAX` and the presence half begins demanding that the RECOMPUTED figure be
written into a paragraph describing what happened on 2026-09-02, when the figure that day was 4,227;
the absence half simultaneously begins asserting the absence of a string that was never in the
docblock — verbatim the failure its own comment says it exists to avoid. The drift detector becomes
the drift generator.

It is LATENT, not live: correct at HEAD and green in the verifier's run. It is also this round's one
recorded `coincidental_reliance_item` (`undeclared-precondition`), which is exactly what it is — the
gate silently depends on a constant not having moved, and never says so.

Purpose. This is the second and last expansion of 07-23's tracer pattern, and the only one of round
3's four gaps that changes an executable file. The other three were prose. This one is a structural
repair to a guard, and the guard's own property — that it fails when the docblock region is emptied —
is the thing most easily lost while repairing it. So the plan demonstrates that property against the
real file rather than asserting it.

Output: two named literals a reader can date, an assertion that carries its own remedy, and the
non-vacuity trap still armed.
</objective>
