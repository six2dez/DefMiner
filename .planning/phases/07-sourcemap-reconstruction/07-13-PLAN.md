<objective>
Give the two tables Phase 7 created a retention sweep, in the eviction order the operator chose at UAT,
and land the A8 cost measurement as a committed benchmark rather than a table in a SUMMARY.

This closes **UAT gap 1** (with **W-5** as a consequence) and **UAT gap 2**. They ship together because
they are the same module and one test run: the benchmark has nothing to measure until the sweep can
delete from these tables, and the sweep has no cost evidence until the benchmark exists.

Purpose. `store/retention.ts` names neither `sources` nor `source_sightings`; migration `v: 8` declares
no foreign key and no `ON DELETE CASCADE`. So deleting an artifact orphans its sightings and both
tables grow with nothing able to delete from them. That also hollows out **W-5**: HI-04's convergence
arithmetic counts inserts into two tables the sweep structurally cannot reach, so the inequality is
satisfied numerically while the property it claims — the database does not grow monotonically past the
retention ceiling — does not hold.

**The eviction order was decided by the operator at UAT and is not open here: CASCADE — a source dies
with its last sighting.** Sweep `source_sightings` first by the normal age and row caps, then delete
`sources` rows with no surviving sighting by ANTI-JOIN. Not a foreign key: an FK is a second schema
change and is not approved.

Output: a sweep whose coverage matches its cadence, and a committed benchmark that asserts bounds.
</objective>
