<objective>
Make a recovered-source sighting's identity NAME ITS BUNDLE, at every layer above the schema.

This is the half of finding **W-3** that is correct under the shipped `v: 8` key and REQUIRED under
the `v: 9` key plan 07-12 asks the operator to approve. It ships first, on its own, so that when the
primary key widens there is no commit at which `readSightingOrigin` can match two rows and
`stmt.get` returns whichever one SQLite reaches first.

Purpose: today `SourceRef` is `{ projectId, mapSha256, sourceIndex }` and `SIGHTING_ORIGIN_SQL` /
`MARK_PRODUCIBILITY_SQL` bind exactly those three. Under `v: 8` that is unambiguous only because the
attribution guard makes a second bundle's sighting unwritable. Take the guard away — which is
precisely what 07-12 does — and those statements become multi-match reads and multi-row writes
against a key they no longer span.

Output: `SourceRef` grows `artifactSha256`; the two single-sighting statements bind it; the RPC, the
client mirror and the drill-down carry it; and the D-24 rationale that currently argues for its
ABSENCE is rewritten to argue what actually holds.
</objective>
