<objective>
Wire reconstruction into the pipeline that already exists, and pay the two debts doing so creates.

D-08 says reconstruction is a stage inside the bounded consumer, always on, no toggle — inheriting
everything the pipeline already guarantees. RESEARCH found the one structural fact that changes where
that stage actually goes: `visit` is synchronous and cannot `await`, and a sourcemap is not a
per-window object. The stage belongs in `analyseAndFinish` after the walk returns.

Two debts come with it, and both reach outside this phase. **O-05:** with `admit()` bypassed,
`REJECT_REASONS` no longer covers everything the pipeline refuses, so a sibling vocabulary is defined
and the reason two are correct is written down. **Pitfall 2:** D-09's one-row-per-source accounting
breaks a shipped, mechanically-asserted convergence inequality by up to 521x, and the fix is a change
to Phase 1 machinery that must be called out rather than slipped in.

Purpose: the passive-pipeline thesis applied to source — nothing manual, nothing optional.
Output: the stage, the sibling vocabulary, the counters, the depth bound, and a sweep cadence that
bounds the database again.
</objective>
