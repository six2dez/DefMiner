  is not.

only on a constant re-measurement nobody has scheduled. Neither can leak, corrupt, crash or change an
exported byte. What they can do is make a guard demand that the repository falsify its own dated
record, and this plan removes the last two ways it could.

The plan reuses 07-25's mechanism rather than inventing a second one — the same named-literal shape,
the same coincidence assertion, the same `region()` predicates, the same `git checkout --` restore —
and adds one thing 07-25 did not need: a SECOND arm-check. Emptying the whole history paragraph proves
the guard fails; it does not prove the *newly substituted* operand is what makes it fail. Removing only
the newly-pinned figure does.

pinned end to end, and a non-vacuity guard demonstrated load-bearing on both of its operands.
