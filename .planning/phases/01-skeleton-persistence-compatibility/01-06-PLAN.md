legibly, and prove every SDK surface it depends on still behaves on the release that shipped after Phase 0
measured everything.

rather than obscure.

recorded three-leg surface matrix.

    build suffix on a segment compares on that segment's numeric core. A segment with no leading digit at all
    makes the whole version unparseable, and an unparseable version is reported as such rather than being
    allowed to propagate NaN through a comparison that then silently returns false everywhere.

    than an assumption.
