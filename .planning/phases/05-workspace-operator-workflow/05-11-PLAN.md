<objective>
Serialise the inventory to both formats on the backend with the shared field rule and the embedded
floor statement, carry it across the remote-procedure boundary, and assemble it into a browser
download on the operator's own machine — with redacted pre-selected and raw gated behind a second
choice and a destructive confirmation.

Purpose: decision D-04 removes the export path from the deployment phase's problem space entirely
rather than deferring it. Nothing to quota, nothing to clean up, nothing to label, identical
behaviour on desktop, remote and containerised installs, and a raw export never touches shared
server disk. The cost it accepts is that the export size is bounded by what crosses the boundary in
memory, which plan 05-02 measured and turned into a constant.

The second half is the safety contract's third rule made real: formula neutralisation before
quoting, in that order, because quoting alone does not stop a spreadsheet.

Output: `export.ts` and its endpoint, `ExportDialog.vue`, and the toolbar action.
</objective>
