<objective>
Build the Scan tab's operator surface: the start form, the progress readout, the lifecycle controls
and the discard confirmation — the half of D-13 that turns the tracer's counter strip into something
an operator can actually run a backfill from.

Purpose: FIND-04 asks that a retroactive scan report progress and be cancellable. Plan 06-01 proved
the path end to end with one page and a four-cell strip; plans 06-03 and 06-05 filled in the
backpressure gate and the lifecycle transitions behind it. What does not exist yet is the surface
where an operator reads any of it. **This is the hardest copy problem in the phase**: a readout with
no fraction must read as progress rather than as a stalled screen, on a job that runs for hours, on a
backend whose single thread genuinely does stop for seconds at a time.

Output: `scan-contract.ts` carrying every string and the DefMiner-authored date formatter,
`ScanPanel.vue` carrying the two mutually exclusive states of one question, `ScanPanel.spec.ts`, the
hostile-corpus extension for the operator's clause, and amendment A1 applied upstream.

**What this plan does NOT own, stated so nobody builds it twice.** The scan history list, the per-scan
detail disclosure and the toolbar indicator are plan 06-13's. The lifecycle badge, the presentation
map and the progress store are plan 06-09's and are consumed here, not rebuilt.
</objective>
