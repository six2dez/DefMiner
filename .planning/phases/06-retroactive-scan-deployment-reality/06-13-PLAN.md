<objective>
Finish D-13: the scan history list with its inline per-scan detail, and the toolbar indicator that
makes a running or suspended scan visible from every tab.

Purpose: FIND-04's operator-visible surface is not complete without the two things that make a scan
recoverable rather than merely watchable. **The toolbar indicator is the one that carries the most
weight, and its load-bearing case is `suspended`, not `running`** — every non-operator route to
suspension (an epoch change, a restart, a retention eviction) produces a scan the operator did not
stop and would otherwise never learn had stopped. A suspended scan invisible from four of five tabs is
exactly the frozen-looking page D-13 exists to prevent, one level up. The history list is where that
scan's row — which IS its cursor — remains findable.

Output: `ScanHistoryList.vue` and its spec, the list mounted as the Scan tab's last block, the toolbar
indicator in `App.vue`, the two remaining hostile-corpus surfaces, and amendment A4 applied upstream.

**One thing this plan must not do, stated because the shipped code makes it tempting.** The shipped
table contract is the repo's answer to every list — and it is mechanically incapable of expressing
this one. `assertColumnContract` throws unless exactly one column is target-controlled, and the scan
history has zero: lifecycle state, counters, the position date and the operator's own clause are all
DefMiner-authored or operator-authored. Marking a column target-controlled to get past the assertion
would be lying to a gate. This list is an authored list in the health panel's idiom, and A4 records
that in the upstream contract so the next author does not rediscover it the wrong way.
</objective>
